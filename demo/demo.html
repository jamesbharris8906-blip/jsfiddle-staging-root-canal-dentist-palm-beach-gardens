Questions People Ask

How long does a visit take?

Most visits run about an hour, though a first visit can run a bit longer because of the talk and the check. The team will tell you the time when you book, so you can plan your day.

Does it hurt?

Most people feel only a little pinch or pressure, and it fades fast. The team uses gentle steps and numbing where it helps, and you can ask them to slow down at any time.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on what you need, so the team gives you a clear quote after your first check. Ask about payment choices when you call, and get it in writing.

How soon will I see a change?

Some changes show right away, while others take days or weeks as your body heals and settles. The team will tell you what to watch for and when to come back.

How often should I come back?

That depends on your plan. Some people come back in a few weeks, others in a few months. The team will set a pace that fits your goals and your life.

Do I need to book ahead?

Booking ahead is best, as good times fill fast. Call or book online, and ask about same-week spots if you need one soon.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring a list of medicines you take, any past notes, and your questions. A photo ID and your payment card help too.

How to Get Ready for Your Visit

A little prep can make your visit feel calm from start to end. The night before, put your ID, your payment card, and your list of questions in one spot by the door. Write down the top three things you want from the visit, and bring that paper with you. If you feel nervous, tell the front desk when you arrive. They hear it all the time, and they can give you a few extra minutes to settle in. Eat a light meal, drink water, and arrive a few minutes early so you can fill in forms without a rush.
